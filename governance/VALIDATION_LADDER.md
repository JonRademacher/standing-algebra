# Validation Ladder

0. Proposed
1. Source-grounded
2. Internally coherent
3. Counterexample-tested
4. Implemented
5. Executed
6. Computationally verified
7. Cross-implementation reproduced
8. Independently reviewed
9. Independently replicated
10. Externally validated

Verification at one level does not imply any higher level.

For formal mathematics, computational verification includes the exact Lean,
Coq, or Z3 invocation and receipt; it does not imply source fidelity,
originality, independent review, empirical adequacy, or external validation.
