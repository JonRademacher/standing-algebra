# No-Regression

Every promoted change names its checkpoint identity, affected dimensions,
evidence for invalidation/reopening, prior-state preservation, rollback
instructions, regression tests, updated residuals, and provenance.

Do not replace a validated checkpoint with an older or weaker realization
without evidence-backed invalidation, an explicitly scoped experiment, or a
witnessed reopening condition. Preserve failed branches and prior checkpoints.
When no candidates survive, localize the obstruction and recover; do not infer
global objective failure.
