# Operator-transfer verdict

The three operator structures inhabit related Adele/idele-class geometry, but related geometry does not imply operator identity.

A valid transfer requires one of:

1. exact equality after typed identification of carriers;
2. unitary equivalence with a displayed intertwiner;
3. compression/restriction with trace-class inheritance;
4. a trace-norm limit theorem;
5. an independently proved exact trace identity for Li's `T_h`.

No such witness has been reconstructed in the current evidence. Therefore Connes's S-local asymptotic formula and Meyer's virtual-character theorem cannot be used as drop-in proofs of Li's candidate Theorem 1.1.
