# Historical critique context

Connes and Consani's *The Scaling Hamiltonian* analyzes an earlier attempt by Xian-Jin Li at Weil positivity and explains its limitation. This is important prior criticism but cannot automatically refute the 2025 v10 manuscript because the manuscript has undergone many revisions and claims a newer trace decomposition.

Correct use:

- treat the critique as a failure-family warning;
- compare the criticized operator step against v10 line by line;
- do not transfer an old refutation to a revised proof without identity evidence.
