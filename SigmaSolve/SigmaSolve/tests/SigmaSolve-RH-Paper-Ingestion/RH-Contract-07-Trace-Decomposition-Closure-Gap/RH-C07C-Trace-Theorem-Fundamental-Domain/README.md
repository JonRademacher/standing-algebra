# RH-C07C — Trace-Theorem Instantiation and Fundamental-Domain Binding

This is a scoped continuation under Contract 07. It is not Contract 08.

## Objective

Bind the external Adele-class construction from Connes to the candidate trace framework, settle the fundamental-domain prerequisites, and determine whether T3 can advance to an actual operator calculation.

## Outcome

- The S-local quotient Hilbert space and fundamental-domain construction are supported by Connes's primary source.
- Connes proves an S-local cutoff trace formula for operators of the form `R_Lambda U(h)` on `L2(X_S)`.
- This does **not** directly instantiate Li's different operator `T_h` or prove Li's exact compressed/cross trace formulas.
- Therefore the fundamental-domain and quotient-carrier gaps narrow substantially, but the trace-class binding for Li's `T_h` remains open.
