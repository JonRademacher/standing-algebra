## What would solve the transformed problem

It is enough to prove a **no-birth theorem** for the defect: `O` starts at zero and can never jump. Merely restating bounded zero exclusion does not prove this. A difference-making route needs a second structure that detects an off-line jump and independently forbids it.

The selected candidate is a constructive positivity contradiction:

1. assume a first off-line orbit at height `T*`;
2. construct from it an admissible test object whose Weil quadratic form is negative, or a Li coefficient that becomes negative;
3. derive nonnegativity of the same object from an independent prime/explicit-formula argument;
4. conclude that the first jump cannot exist.

Li's criterion is an exact positivity equivalence, and Weil's criterion likewise converts RH into positive-definiteness. Those criteria identify the right shape, but the missing proof is universal positivity itself. The useful new SigmaSolve target is therefore not `B(T)` again. It is the **witness constructor** from a hypothetical first off-line zero to explicit negative finite evidence, plus an independent nonnegativity theorem for that evidence.
