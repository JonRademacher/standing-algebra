# Analytic binding obligations

The abstract finite theorem is not yet a zeta theorem. For each T>0, formalize a finite multiplicity-aware multiset containing exactly the nontrivial zeros rho of `riemannZeta` with `0 < rho.im <= T`. Prove finiteness, soundness, completeness, exact multiplicity, critical-line filtering, and boundary conventions. Then prove correspondence of its cardinalities with N(T) and N0(T), and finally prove `RiemannHypothesis <-> forall T>0, N0(T)=N(T)`.

The final universal equality remains the open RH burden.
